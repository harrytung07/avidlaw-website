List of Common Client Misconceptions (Prenuptial Agreements)
  常见客户户误区清单（婚前协议篇）
  
  1. Regarding Children: A misconception is believing you have no financial obligation to a partner's children from a previous relationship.
  子女相关：误区是以为一方从前一段关系带来的子女，自己没有抚养义务。
  
  Reality: Under B.C.'s Family Law Act, if you act as a parent to your partner's child during your relationship, you may be required to pay child support upon separation.
  现实：根据 B.C. 省《家庭法》，如果在同居期间，一方实质上扮演了另一方子女的父母角色，在分居时可能需要承担抚养费义务。
  
  2. Increase in Property Value: A misconception is assuming that pre-relationship property and any growth in its value will automatically remain separate.
  财产增值：误区是认为婚前个人财产永远是独立的，其增值部分也无需在协议中提及。
  
  Reality: In B.C., the increase in the value of an asset that occurs during the relationship is presumptively considered family property.
  现实：在 B.C. 省，婚前资产在关系存续期间的增值部分，默认为家庭财产。
  
  3. Foreign Assets: A misconception is thinking that assets located outside of Canada do not need to be disclosed.
  海外资产：误区是认为境外资产不需要向对方披露。
  
  Reality: Full and honest financial disclosure is the foundation of a valid agreement.
  现实：完整、诚实的财务披露是协议有效性的基石。
  
  4. Income and Future Changes: A misconception is believing that current income does not need to be disclosed because it is subject to change.
  收入与未来变化：误区是认为当前的收入不必披露，因为它将来会改变。
  
  Reality: A complete picture of your financial circumstances at the time of signing, including income, must be disclosed.
  现实：签署协议时的财务状况（包括收入）必须全面披露。
  
  5. Waiver of Spousal Support: A misconception is believing that signing a spousal support waiver is a "100% risk-free" guarantee.
  放弃配偶赡养费：误区是相信只要签署了放弃赡养费的条款，就"百分之百无风险"。
  
  Reality: A court may still intervene if a significant change in circumstances makes the waiver significantly unfair in the future.
  现实：如果未来情况发生极端变化导致协议条款变得极度不公平时，法院仍有权介入。
  
  6. The Family Home: A misconception is thinking that if a property is registered only in one partner's name, it cannot be considered the "family home."
  家庭住宅：误区是认为只要房产仅在一方名下，就自动不属于"家庭住宅"。
  
  Reality: In B.C., a property that is ordinarily occupied by the couple is often treated as the family home, regardless of whose name is on the title.
  现实：在 B.C. 省，无论房产证上是谁的名字，只要夫妻双方通常居住于此，该房产就可能被认定为家庭住宅。
  
  7. Gifts and Inheritances: A misconception is assuming that personal gifts or inheritances do not need to be addressed in the agreement.
  礼物与继承：误区是以为个人获得的继承或赠与财产无需在协议中处理。
  
  Reality: While generally excluded property, they can be converted into family property if commingled into joint accounts or used to purchase joint assets.
  现实：虽然这些资产原则上是独立财产，但如果将其存入联名账户或用来购买共同房产，就可能被"转化"为家庭财产。
  
  8. Invincibility of the Agreement: A misconception is thinking that once an agreement is signed, it is "ironclad" and cannot be challenged.
  协议的绝对性：误区是以为协议一旦签署，就"牢不可破"，在任何情况下都不能被挑战。
  
  Reality: A court can set aside or modify an agreement if it was procured unfairly, such as through a lack of independent legal advice or under duress.
  现实：如果协议缺乏独立的法律意见、未进行完整的财务披露，或在签署时存在压力、胁迫等不公平情况，法院未来可能会判定协议无效。
